About the Ministry

Growing in God's Word

The Health Club at CSI Church, Aruvikkara, is dedicated to encouraging physical well-being, healthy living, and wholesome fellowship among church members. Recognizing that caring for one’s body goes hand in hand with spiritual growth, the club convenes every Sunday following the morning worship service, from 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM. Through guided fitness activities, health awareness, and mutual encouragement, this ministry provides a vibrant platform for parishioners to stay active, energetic, and mindful of healthy habits together.
