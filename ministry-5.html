About the Ministry

Growing in God's Word

The Church Choir at CSI Church, Aruvikkara, plays a vital role in leading the congregation into heartfelt worship and praise. Blessed with a dedicated team of 35 Senior Choir members and 13 vibrant Junior Choir members, the ministry beautifully unites generations through sacred music. To prepare diligently for Sunday services and special occasions, both groups gather faithfully for practice every Saturday from 6:00 PM to 7:00 PM, nurturing musical talents and enriching the spiritual life of the parish.
