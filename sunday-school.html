About the Ministry

Growing in God's Word

Grounded in God’s timeless word, the Sunday School ministry at LM CSI Church, Aruvikkara, is a joyful home of learning and fellowship for more than 255 eager students. Guided by the selfless dedication of 32 passionate teachers, our classrooms come alive every Sunday right after the morning service, from 10:00 AM to 11:00 AM. Catering to everyone from little toddlers in Nursery to our elders in Super Adult II, the sessions make Bible study enriching, relatable, and transformative for every generation. Together, we take immense joy in raising a generation deeply rooted in the Scriptures, growing daily in grace, love, and Christian purpose.
