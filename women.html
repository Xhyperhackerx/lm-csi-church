About the Ministry

Growing in God's Word

The Christian Endeavor Junior Fellowship (CEJF) at CSI Church, Aruvikkara, is a vibrant and active fellowship of around 75 young members. Gathering every Sunday immediately following the main worship service, from 11:00 AM to 12:00 PM, the fellowship offers a dedicated space for children and youth to grow spiritually. Through engaging Bible studies, action songs, prayer, and interactive activities, CEJF nurtures Christian values, leadership, and lasting bonds of friendship among the parish youth.
