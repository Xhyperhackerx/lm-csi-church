About the Ministry

Growing in God's Word

The Christ Evangelical Youth Fellowship (CEYF) at CSI Church, Aruvikkara, is a vibrant and growing community of 83 active young members. Meeting every Sunday right after the morning service from 11:00 AM to 12:00 PM, the fellowship offers a welcoming space for youth to gather, worship, study Scripture, and strengthen their faith together. It serves as an encouraging environment for fellowship, personal growth, and meaningful connection within the church family.
