About the Ministry

Growing in God's Word

The Women’s Fellowship at CSI Church, Aruvikkara, is a devoted and inspiring ministry comprising 65 active members. Meeting every Sunday right after the morning worship service, from 11:00 AM to 12:00 PM, the fellowship serves as a pillar of spiritual support, prayer, and sisterhood within the parish. Through shared Scripture reading, intercessory prayer, and compassionate outreach, the members continue to nurture Christian faith, strengthen family life, and serve the broader community with grace.
